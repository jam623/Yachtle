How to Play

Roll the dice up to 3 times per turn to score points in different categories.

Choose a category on your scorecard to lock in your points for that turn and try to get enough points to match or exceed the goal score of the day!

Category Scoring Reference

  • Ones – Sixes: Sum of dice matching that number.
  • Three of a Kind: At least 3 matching dice. Scores the sum of ALL 5 dice.
  • Four of a Kind: At least 4 matching dice. Scores the sum of ALL 5 dice.
  • Full House: 3 of one number and 2 of another. Scores a flat 25 points.
  • Small Straight: 4 sequential dice. Scores a flat 30 points.
  • Large Straight: 5 sequential dice. Scores a flat 40 points.
  • Yacht: All 5 dice showing the same number. Always in effect even if not a category that day. Scores a flat 50 points.
  • Chance: No requirements. Scores the sum of ALL 5 dice.

💡 Tip: You can lock in zero points for a category if you roll poorly, but strategic picks keep you alive!
